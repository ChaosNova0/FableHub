# FableHub 0.28.1

### Install
1. Close the game, then delete your old `Interface\AddOns\FableHub` folder.
2. Unzip so that `Interface\AddOns\FableHub\FableHub.toc` exists.
3. Your settings and guild data are kept.

It syncs with 0.27.x and 0.28.0 as normal.

### New: a FableHub tab on the Guild & Communities window
- The game's Guild & Communities window now has a **FableHub** tab under the tabs down its right-hand side. Click it to swap that window for FableHub.
- It only shows while you're in a guild. Don't want it? Turn it off in Settings > General.

### Fixed
- 0.28.0 showed a "Couldn't find inherited node" error at login, and its tab on the Social window never appeared. That tab is gone; the FableHub tab now lives on the Guild & Communities window instead.
