# FableHub 0.29.0

### Install
1. Close the game, then delete your old `Interface\AddOns\FableHub` folder.
2. Unzip so that `Interface\AddOns\FableHub\FableHub.toc` exists.
3. Your settings and guild data are kept.

It syncs with 0.27.x and 0.28.x as normal.

### New: Craftsman's Writs
- A new page under **Professions** lists all 150 Craftsman's Writs on Forever. Search by the writ or the item it asks for, or pick a profession and level (Journeyman, Expert, Artisan).
- Hover a writ to see the item it asks for, so you can tell straight away if it binds when picked up. It also shows the skill needed, the reputation, who to deliver it to (where that's known) and which guildmates can craft it.
- Writs in your quest log are marked **Mine**. A shopping list shows what they need and how many you have in your bags.
- At the auction house, **Search AH** looks up one item per click. Right-click a writ to search for the writ itself or its item. FableHub never buys anything for you.
- Right-click a writ to ask the guild to craft its item. This opens the Craft Request form already filled in, ready for you to post. You can also whisper a guildmate who can craft it.

### Removed
- The Camping guide and Camp planner are gone. Any camp plans you saved are no longer shown.
