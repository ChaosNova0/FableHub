# FableHub 0.30.0

### Install
1. Close the game, then delete your old `Interface\AddOns\FableHub` folder.
2. Unzip so that `Interface\AddOns\FableHub\FableHub.toc` exists.
3. Your settings and guild data are kept.

It syncs with 0.27.x, 0.28.x and 0.29.x as normal.

### New: resizable columns
- Every table with column titles (Craft Requests, Members, Loot, Guild Bank, Recipes and the rest) now lets you resize its columns. Drag the thin line after a column's title.
- Your widths are remembered for each table, even after you log out.
- Double-click a line to put that column back. Right-click any column title and pick **Reset column widths** to reset the whole table.
