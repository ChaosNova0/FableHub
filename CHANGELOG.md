# FableHub 0.28.0

### Install
1. Close the game, then delete your old `Interface\AddOns\FableHub` folder.
2. Unzip so that `Interface\AddOns\FableHub\FableHub.toc` exists.
3. Your settings and guild data are kept.

It syncs with 0.27.x as normal.

### New: a FableHub tab on the Social window
- The game's Social window (O) now has a **FableHub** tab after Friends, Who, Guild and Raid. Click it to swap the Social window for FableHub.
- It only shows while you're in a guild. Don't want it? Turn it off in Settings > General.
